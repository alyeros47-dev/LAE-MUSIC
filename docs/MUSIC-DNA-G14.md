# G14 — Francophone African Gospel

Instruction Package 15 adds five individually selectable profiles beneath the
nonselectable **Francophone African Gospel** heading, with exact supplied public names
and descriptions. The catalogue now contains 65 styles across G1–G5 and G7–G14.
G6 — Indigenous Igbo Gospel remains the one unsupplied Gospel group; the Gospel
catalogue is not represented as complete.

| ID | Name | BPM | Defining identity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G14-01 | Heavenly River Worship | 66–92 | Soulful Congolese worship, smooth melodic bass, warm keyboards, subtle guitar phrases and expressive choir |
| G14-02 | Congo Glory Celebration | 104–132 | Energetic Congolese praise, interlocking soukous-influenced guitars, bouncy bass and powerful choir responses |
| G14-03 | Kingdom Afro-Gospel Pulse | 102–128 | Ivorian urban Gospel, electronic/electric bass, syncopated rhythms and bright contemporary synth hooks |
| G14-04 | Graceful African Soul | 76–104 | Gentle Francophone Afro-soul, R&B phrasing, warm Rhodes/grand piano, smooth bass and restrained dance percussion |
| G14-05 | Sacred Congo Encounter | 60–86 | Deep Congolese prayer, sustained bass, atmospheric keys, gradual choir development and controlled final intensity |

Profiles preserve supplied rhythms, drum/bass/keyboard DNA, guitars, additional
instruments, melody, mood, arrangement, lyrics and production emphasis. Bounded
variations change approved riffs, voicings, responses, bass grooves and instrumental
transitions while preserving each Congolese/Ivorian identity. Profiles assign no lead
gender, voice is independently selected and no mood selector is added. Internal research
references remain excluded from public metadata and provider prompts.

French and English, including their bilingual combination, are supported independently
of the style. Existing Pidgin, Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa and supported combinations remain
available. Francophone identity never forces French. Lingala is not currently selectable
or verified in the shared language registry: “when supported” does not activate that
capability. Future Lingala integration needs a shared language addition and focused
linguistic/provider checks; no unsupported translations are promised.

`npm run test:dna -- --groups G14` passes 14 focused G14 tests and 7 CI-routing checks.
Tests cover exact metadata and tempos, individual selection and nonselectable heading,
private references, distinct musical cores, fixed mood, voice/language independence,
French/English/bilingual requests, bounded variation and stable retries. Actual existing
lyrics-handler calls use synthetic provider replies and verify one credit reservation/
completion per success. Production text stays within the existing 950-character limit.
All 60 earlier profiles remain unchanged; no shared runtime, provider, voice, billing or
financial code changed. Full earlier-group, unrelated platform, database and browser
suites are not repeated.

No paid generation, real audio verification, merge or deployment occurred. Text contracts
do not certify audio originality, cultural fidelity, linguistic quality or audible style
differentiation. Authorized provider/listening evaluation remains for final integration.
