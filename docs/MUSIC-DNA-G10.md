# G10 — Ghanaian Deep Worship

Instruction Package 11 adds five individually selectable profiles under the
nonselectable **Ghanaian Deep Worship** heading, with exact supplied customer names
and descriptions. The library contains 45 styles across G1–G5 and G7–G10; G6 remains
pending its approved specification.

| ID | Name | BPM | Defining identity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G10-01 | Intimate Mercy Worship | 62–82 | Soulful intimate grand/electric piano, rounded sustained bass, gentle drums and gradual emotional development |
| G10-02 | Heritage Devotion | 76–100 | Gentle highlife guitar interplay, flowing bass passing notes, soft organ and thoughtful devotional storytelling |
| G10-03 | Altar of Thanksgiving | 70–94 | Moderate live worship, expressive Gospel piano, deep melodic bass and gradually expanded choir harmonies |
| G10-04 | Prayer of Restoration | 58–80 | Solemn spacious prayer, minimal introductory percussion, sustained bass and a powerful but controlled final chorus |
| G10-05 | Presence and Peace | 64–86 | Natural acoustic worship, understated bass, soft piano, ambient guitars and a peaceful closing refrain |

Each profile contains its supplied mood, drum/bass/keyboard DNA, additional instruments,
rhythm, arrangement, lyrical themes and bounded variation directions. Mixing instructions
preserve Ghanaian worship characteristics and differences between soulful piano, gentle
highlife, choir testimony, solemn prayer and intimate acoustic worship. Heritage Devotion
explicitly avoids becoming fast celebratory praise. Voice and supported languages remain
independent; profile delivery is gender-neutral and no mood selector is added. Research
references remain internal and excluded from public metadata and provider instructions.

Existing supported English, Pidgin, Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, French and combinations work
with all five profiles. Twi is not currently selectable or verified; the package's
“when available” language clause does not activate a new capability. Profiles never
force Twi or invent unsupported translations. Twi needs a future shared language
addition and focused linguistic/provider evaluation.

`npm run test:dna -- --groups G10` passes 14 focused G10 tests and 7 CI-routing checks.

heading, private references, distinct instrumental/arrangement cores, prompt construction,

reservation/completion per success. All 40 earlier profiles remain unchanged; no shared
runtime, voice, provider, billing or financial code changed. Broad earlier-group,
unrelated platform, database and browser tests are not repeated.

No paid provider calls, real audio verification, merge or deployment occurred. Text
