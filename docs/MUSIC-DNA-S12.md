## Cultural and provider boundaries

S12-02 is a cross-regional East/Central African fusion, not a claim of Francophone origin,
traditional Congolese rumba or authentic Ivorian coupé-décalé. The internal reference's
Tanzanian Bongo Flava tradition is not reclassified here; its core belongs to the pending
S14 specification. Research references remain private and excluded from public metadata
and provider prompts.

S12-01 uses the existing single base-tempo contract at **96–118 BPM**. The approved
optional **120–136 BPM** dance-section direction is retained in arrangement/mixing
instructions only where section-tempo changes are supported; otherwise the prompt keeps
base tempo and increases rhythmic density. This adds no provider parameter and does not
verify that the audio provider can execute intra-song tempo changes. Real delivery of
that optional transition remains pending provider/listening verification.

Mood is internal; voices and supported languages remain independent. French, English
and their bilingual combination are supported alongside existing Pidgin, Yoruba, Igbo,
Hausa and supported combinations. French is never forced. Lingala remains unavailable/
unverified in the shared registry and needs a later shared-language integration plus
linguistic/provider checks. No unsupported translations are promised.
