arrangement, lyrical themes, predefined mood/energy and production safeguards.

lyrics. Artist research references remain internal; public options and provider
instructions exclude them and prohibit identifiable imitation. Voice selection does
not change instrumental DNA or assign a default gender. A Tanzanian style never
forces Swahili or overrides the selected supported language.
