# G6 — Indigenous Igbo Gospel

Instruction Package 16 adds five selectable profiles under the nonselectable
**Indigenous Igbo Gospel** heading. G6 is inserted between G5 and G7 in both category
and style order, not appended after G14. The catalogue contains all 14 supplied Gospel
groups and 70 styles; this is registration completeness, not audio or release certification.

| ID | Name | BPM | Defining identity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G6-01 | Igbo Thanksgiving Highlife | 108–134 | Prominent interlocking guitars and bouncy highlife bass, naturally supporting ogene/ekwe/udu, choir responses and an extended ending |
| G6-02 | Heritage Gospel Procession | 100–126 | Measured indigenous percussion cycles, narrative verses, restrained keys and communal response refrains |
| G6-03 | Fire of Igbo Praise | 118–145 | Powerful fast live drums, punchy highlife bass, energetic ogene/ekwe, congas and passionate choir responses |
| G6-04 | Eastern Gospel Highlife Revival | 106–132 | Classic flowing lead/rhythm guitars, walking bass transitions, bright organ and extended guitar-and-bass interplay |
| G6-05 | Sacred Eastern Testimony | 78–108 | Mature reflective storytelling, gentle highlife, understated bass, light ogene/udu and warm soft communal harmonies |

Profiles retain exact public names/descriptions and approved tempo, mood, rhythm,
drum/bass/keyboard DNA, guitars, instruments, arrangements, lyric themes and variation
directions. Ogene is a metal gong, ekwe a wooden slit drum and udu a clay drum; their
roles and intensity differ by style. Fire of Igbo Praise does not add an unrequested
udu part and keeps fast, strong percussion. Sacred Eastern Testimony uses lighter
percussion and mature storytelling. Mixing instructions preserve southeastern Nigerian
musical identity instead of generic Afrobeats, Western worship or Yoruba praise.

Voice and supported languages remain independent and no gender is assigned to profile
delivery. Igbo, English, Nigerian Pidgin and bilingual combinations are tested; Igbo
musical identity never forces Igbo lyrics. Mood is internal. Research names and aliases
remain private and are excluded from public metadata and provider instructions.

`npm run test:dna -- --groups G6` passes 15 focused G6/completeness tests and 7 CI-routing
checks. It covers individual selection, private references, exact metadata/tempos,
distinct instrumental cores, fixed mood, voice/language independence, bounded variation,
retry stability and actual lyrics-handler prompt construction with synthetic replies.
Successful calls reserve/complete one credit. The lightweight completeness test verifies
G1–G14 in numeric order, five unique correctly categorized style IDs each, 70 total and
G6's rendered selector position. It does not rerun earlier generation suites.
