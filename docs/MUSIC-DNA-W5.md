# W5 — Western Pop & Dance

Instruction Package 42 adds five independently selectable profiles beneath the
nonselectable **Western and International / Western Pop & Dance** heading after W1–W4.

| ID | Style | BPM | Distinct musical fingerprint |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| W5-01 | Royal Pop Rhythm | 100–124 | Punchy pop-R&B drums, marching-style snare accents, electric/synth bass octaves, rhythmic piano, funk guitar and brass stabs |
| W5-02 | Caribbean Pop Fire | 98–124 | Caribbean syncopated synth bass, tropical conga/shaker percussion, synth plucks and subtle offbeat guitar |
| W5-03 | Neon Disco Revival | 112–128 | Four-on-the-floor drums, actively melodic electric bass/octaves, analog synths, disco strings and muted sixteenth-note funk guitar |

| W5-05 | Starlight Pop R&B | 94–118 | Smooth polished electronic drums, electric/synth bass slides, bright electric piano/plucks and tasteful voice-adapted ornamentation |

Profiles preserve supplied names/descriptions, tempos, embedded moods, drum/bass/
keyboard/guitar DNA, additional instruments, melody, rhythm, arrangements and
production safeguards. Each keeps its own rhythmic identity rather than generic
festival EDM or trap. Clear powerful bass, punchy kicks, crisp vocals and commercial
instrumental separation remain priorities. Bounded variation develops permitted
introductions, bass grooves, drum breaks, synth/keyboard hooks, guitar/brass/string
parts, arrangements and original melodic/lyrical expression, with stable retries.

Customer topics are authoritative. Voice, gender, supported languages and harmony
settings remain independent. Caribbean Pop never forces Jamaican accents, and
Starlight adapts range/ornamentation to selected voice capability without mandatory
high pitch or excessive runs. Research references stay private and excluded from
public metadata/provider prompts; identifiable imitation is prohibited.
Existing English, Nigerian Pidgin, Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, French and supported
combinations remain available. Arbitrary custom language codes remain unsupported
pending separate integration and linguistic/provider verification.
