# Approved LAE-MUSIC financial protection and refund policy

Owner-approved on **2026-10-26** in this development session. This supersedes the earlier A/B proposals, including the proposed automatic unused-time Premium proration. **Implementation and verification are in progress; this document does not enable live financial operations or authorize deployment.**

1. **Premium listeners.** Full refunds are allowed for duplicate charges, failed activation or verified service errors. After access begins, voluntary cancellation does not automatically refund unused time, except where required by law. Access remains available until the paid expiration date.
2. **Artist packages.** A full refund is available before activation if no paid benefits have been used. After uploads, AI credits or other paid services have been used, refunds are limited to verified failures, unused refundable benefits or legal requirements. An unsupported partial-benefit calculation must remain under review rather than inventing a price for consumed benefits.
3. **AI generation.** Restore the reserved credit/entitlement only for a verified provider failure with no usable result delivered, and only once. Successfully completed generations are not refundable because a customer dislikes the result. Unknown, timed-out or conflicting outcomes require reconciliation rather than another submission or automatic restoration.
4. **Artist royalties.** Protect legitimate finalized royalties. Refund/chargeback losses should first use appropriate platform reserves. Fraudulent earnings, duplicate payments and accounting errors may be reversed through documented, reviewable adjustments; do not rewrite historical statements or treat an operator assertion as verified evidence.
5. **Company protection.** Maintain reasonable reserves and prevent refund abuse, duplicate claims, fraudulent streams and unauthorized withdrawals. Never promise refunds or payouts beyond verified entitlements. No reserve funding amount or new fee is invented; a reserve is usable money only when independently verified and recorded with its source.
6. **Transparency and disputes.** Disclose conditions before payment. Retain complete original transactions, conversion evidence, policy acknowledgements, decisions and audit history. Provide fair dispute review and comply with applicable consumer protections. An authenticated notice may hold affected unreleased funds; a final outcome requires independent provider evidence and must respect unrelated holds.
7. **Preserved rules.** Keep approved fixed USD prices, existing affiliate percentages, universal 50% Premium streaming royalties, gifts 70/30, withdrawal 3% and the shared international currency/payment safeguards. No permanent listener MP3 exports, paid song purchases or download commissions.

Gateway submission, independent confirmation and accounting are separate operations. A completed external refund must be recorded accurately even if it requires a policy exception investigation. Never represent a refund request, approval, webhook or ambiguous response as returned money. Refunds use the original payment currency and conversion records, bounded by the actual charge; no duplicate conversion or currency mixing.

The owner explicitly instructed: **“Implement and test these policies. Do not activate live financial operations or deploy without my authorization.”** The earlier proposal document is retained as history, not active terms.
