# Approved LAE-MUSIC financial protection and refund policy

Owner-approved correction on **2026-10-26**, revision `owner-financial-2026-10-26-v2`. This replaces the earlier automatic platform-loss allocation and the unused-time proration proposal. M20 implements the seven-day complaint and reconciliation safeguards; real-provider operations remain unavailable. Policy approval does not authorize live finance or deployment.

1. **Complaint window.** Submit an eligible complaint within seven calendar days of the relevant verified payment or service incident. Ordinary voluntary requests close afterward. Applicable law, payment-provider rules and verified fraud can require later review. This deadline does **not** eliminate legally enforceable chargebacks. Filing is not proof of eligibility or a completed refund.
2. **Premium listeners.** Full refunds are allowed for duplicate charges, failed activation or verified service errors. Voluntary cancellation after access begins does not automatically refund unused time, except where required by law. Paid access continues until expiration; automatic renewal is off.
3. **Artist packages.** Full refunds are available before activation if no paid benefits have been used. After uploads, AI credits or other paid services are used, refunds are limited to verified failures, unused refundable benefits or legal requirements. Unsupported partial-benefit valuations remain under review; no invented allocation is charged or refunded.
4. **AI generation.** Restore a reserved credit only for a verified provider failure with no usable result, once only. Successful output is not refundable for dissatisfaction. Unknown, timed-out or contradictory results require reconciliation before retrying or restoring credits.
5. **Monthly royalties.** Calculate the monthly estimate, then allow seven full calendar days after month-end for settlement and reconciliation. Review refunds, chargebacks, fraudulent streams, payment disputes and accounting errors. Valid verified reversals reduce the relevant revenue pool before finalization. Unresolved reviews block finalization; an estimate is not withdrawable cash.
6. **Finalization and release.** Finalize only after the reconciliation period and successful review. Release additionally requires independently verified, sufficient settled funds, matching source/currency evidence and no unresolved hold. A request or approval is not a bank payout.
7. **Later disputes.** Protect legitimate artist earnings and LAE-MUSIC. Handle later losses through documented reserves, legally permitted adjustments, fraud recovery and fair artist agreements. Neither LAE-MUSIC nor artists automatically bear every loss. Historical statements remain immutable. Review notes do not establish funded reserves, legal consent, fraud or a completed financial adjustment. Reserve funding and generic reviewed correction/recovery execution remain unavailable pending independently verified workflows.
8. **Transparency and preservation.** Disclose these conditions before payment and withdrawal. Preserve original charges, currency, USD equivalent, rate/timestamp, approvals, review evidence and append-only history. Refund only in the original payment currency using original conversion records, bounded by the verified charge. Preserve approved USD prices, affiliate commissions, 50% streaming royalties, gifts 70/30 and withdrawal fee 3%. No permanent listener MP3 exports, paid song purchases or download commissions.

Accounting uses **UTC**. Seven calendar days include weekends and holidays. A complaint is timely through the same UTC instant seven days after the verified event (inclusive), not seven business days or seven additional end-of-day grace periods. For September royalties, the period ends October 1 at 00:00 UTC; seven complete reconciliation days finish October 8 at 00:00 UTC. This is the earliest possible finalization/release, never a guaranteed payment date. The 48-hour offline-play synchronization deadline is unchanged and fits inside this hold.

Complaint receipt time comes from the database; a client-entered date cannot reopen an expired window. Staff can record a verified incident linked to the same purchase and supporting evidence. Distinct verified incidents can have distinct complaints; the same claim and all financial refunds remain duplicate-protected. Legal/provider/fraud allegations require documented review and independent evidence before financial action.

Gateway submission, independent confirmation and accounting remain separate. Record actual verified external refunds and chargebacks even when they arrive after the complaint deadline. Never represent a request, review, webhook assertion or ambiguous response as returned money. Preserve historical M19 expense records; new late cases use pending-allocation clearing and documented review, without automatic artist debits or platform expenses.
