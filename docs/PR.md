Commercial contradictions are documented; no recruitment rewards or automatic per-stream revenue are introduced.

USD remains the product/accounting currency. Country defaults, manual currency choices, dated local estimates and immutable FX quotes preserve playback. Nigeria is the confirmed merchant country; the selected gateways are Paystack and Flutterwave v3. A guarded staging coordinator binds each order to one provider, quote and reference, independently verifies the original NGN/USD charge, and atomically records receipts, product benefits, pending commissions and audit evidence. Uncertain initialization retains its reference rather than retrying or switching gateways. The committed manifest keeps payments disabled; adding keys alone cannot activate production or live mode.

Refund requests now connect artist purchase history with protected administrator review. Paystack sandbox refunds reserve one submission attempt, verify the original payment and charge currency, retain uncertainty after a timeout, and require an independent provider lookup before reversing a full sale. Immutable original-currency observations and USD reversal journals preserve paid history. Subscription and purchased-credit holds prevent reuse; full refunds reverse pending commissions. Already-cleared commissions require reconciliation and block USD withdrawal requests and approval of reserved requests. Signed refund/dispute notices only establish review holds; out-of-order notices quarantine an unfulfilled reference. Final upload publication rechecks its entitlement under the same creator lock as refund holds. Partial refunds, consumed services and ambiguous dispute outcomes do not invent proration or success.

Account security now connects separate listener and artist sessions, reauthenticated device/all-session revocation, atomic registration, transactional password changes and stale-password login protection. Recovery and verification use single-use purpose-bound links, an encrypted outbox, durable account limits, configured HTTPS URLs and URL-fragment scrubbing. Competing workers claim a message once; uncertain delivery is not blindly retried. Actual SMTP/TLS fixture checks verify the pinned Nodemailer library, recipient rejection and certificate validation.
