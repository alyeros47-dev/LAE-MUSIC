Refund requests now connect artist purchase history with protected administrator review. Already-cleared commissions require reconciliation and block USD withdrawal requests and approval of reserved requests. Signed refund/dispute notices only establish review holds; out-of-order notices quarantine an unfulfilled reference. Final upload publication rechecks its entitlement under the same creator lock as refund holds. Partial refunds, consumed services and ambiguous dispute outcomes do not invent proration or success.
