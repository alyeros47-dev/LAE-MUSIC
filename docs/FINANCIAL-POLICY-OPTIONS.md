## Proposed shared operating rules

These form part of the proposal and require approval along with the choices above.

1. **Authority and evidence.** Only a recently verified finance/owner operator can approve a refund. An authenticated customer may request one for their own purchase. Separate request, approval, provider submission and verified completion. A gateway webhook or operator note alone never establishes completion. Never claim money has been returned while a provider result is pending or ambiguous.
2. **Amount and currency.** Refund to the original gateway/payment currency and original payer instrument where supported. Reuse the original immutable USD and local-currency conversion evidence; do not reprice with today's rate. Store original charge, cumulative refunds, normalized USD reversal, snapshot/rate/time and provider references. Never exceed the original charge or reverse more than the original USD entitlement. Use integer minor units; a final full refund takes the exact remaining amount to avoid rounding drift. Independently verify gateway support and minimum amounts first.
3. **Proration, if selected.** Preserve the request timestamp. For a current Premium term, use the remaining fraction of that term's exact duration, bounded to [0,1]. Fully future prepaid terms are wholly unused unless already consumed through another benefit. Lock the purchase/entitlement while calculating. A partial refund cannot leave refunded service available: truncate the affected future access and invalidate/revalidate offline licenses as required. Do not revoke unrelated paid terms or free features.
4. **Provider costs and commissions.** Chargebacks/refunds never manufacture revenue. Record actual independently evidenced gateway fees and recoveries in their own currency books, with original conversion records. Reverse only the proportional verified-sale commissions from the affected product; leave artist-package percentages unchanged. Pending commissions stay held. Already-cleared commissions require an explicit recovery rule before implementation; until then hold the case for review and prevent double recovery.
5. **Disputes.** An authenticated signed notice may place the affected purchase and unreleased proceeds on hold. Retrieve the dispute independently from the original merchant account. A verified loss follows the approved refund/correction rules. A verified win may release only the dispute's own hold after all other fraud/refund/reconciliation holds and service eligibility checks pass. Pending, mismatched, unavailable and unknown outcomes remain held. Never issue a second voluntary refund during an unresolved dispute without confirmed provider coordination.
6. **Finalized royalties.** Do not edit or delete source receipts, recognized revenue, allocation records, statements or cash-release history. Link a new correction to the original source and statement. Keep historical NGN books separate from approved USD books. Recognize genuine corrections exactly once, and prevent a refund, dispute and correction from reversing the same amount more than once. Never promise a fixed per-stream price or change the approved 50% rate.
7. **Review history.** Store reason, selected policy revision, operator identity, before/after state, source evidence and immutable idempotent events. Support escalation and customer-visible status without leaking provider secrets or another user's records. No arbitrary balance edits, false payout statuses or retroactive changes to customer contracts.
