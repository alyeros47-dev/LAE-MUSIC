The fundamental distinction is explicit: Fuji's traditional percussion leads; Juju's
clean interlocking guitars lead. Profiles retain supplied drum/bass/keyboard/guitar DNA,
rhythm, melody, mood, arrangement, lyrics and bounded creative variations. Sakara, omele,
agogo, shekere and talking drums develop musically in Fuji rather than becoming generic
effects. Juju preserves melodic electric bass, warm keys and supportive natural percussion.
S8 remains distinct from S5's modern Fuji-influenced Afrobeats: extended traditional
passages, ceremonial conversations and guitar-led Juju are not ordinary electronic beats.

Mood stays internal. Voice and supported language combinations remain independent, with
no lead gender assigned. Yoruba cultural origin never forces Yoruba lyrics; English,
Pidgin, Igbo, Hausa, French and supported combinations work independently. Research
references remain private and are excluded from public metadata and provider prompts.
Only original melodies, refrains, narrative phrases and instrumental responses are requested.
