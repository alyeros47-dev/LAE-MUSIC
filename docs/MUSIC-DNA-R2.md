additional instruments, melody, arrangement, themes and production safeguards.

randomly added instruments. Prominent clean bass, live-band warmth and separation

voicings, rhythmic details, arrangements, melodic contours and original lyrical

Mood remains internal. Voice and supported language settings remain independent.

regional language. Twi needs separate language integration and linguistic/provider
verification.
