# S2 — Smooth Afropop & Romantic

Instruction Package 18 adds five selectable profiles beneath the nonselectable
**Secular and Afrobeats / Smooth Afropop & Romantic** heading, separately from S1 Party
Afrobeats. Exact supplied names and customer descriptions are retained. The library now
contains 80 styles: 70 Gospel and ten Secular/Afrobeats across S1/S2.

Profiles preserve supplied rhythm, drum/bass/keyboard DNA, guitars, melody, arrangement,
mood, lyrical themes, production emphasis and permitted variation directions. Bass,
guitar and percussion remain present and rhythmically engaging without overwhelming
the romantic atmosphere. Approved avoidance directions are explicit in production text:
no explosive party drums for Midnight Afro-Silk, no heavy trap for Sweetheart, no loud
club treatment for Velvet, and no party brass/overly bright arrangement for After-Dark.
These are prompt directions, not certification of actual audio mix or danceability.

Golden Heart's Gospel-influenced chord voicings are harmonic color, not instructions to
change romantic lyrics into worship. No lead gender or vocal register is assigned.
All profiles accept independently selected voices and supported English, Nigerian Pidgin,
Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, French and combinations. Mood remains internal. Research references
stay private and are excluded from public metadata and provider instructions.
