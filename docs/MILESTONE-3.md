# Milestone 3: product purchases and verified accounting

This candidate adds the purchase and entitlement core to the existing application. It does not enable live checkout, accept real payments or send payouts. No production database was accessed. The default server constructs the commerce service without a provider adapter; checkout and public webhook routes still return HTTP 503. A browser flag or environment variable cannot simulate a successful payment.

## Connected behavior

Artists can review approved annual Starter ($6), Growth ($10) and Professional ($20) packages, save an unpaid purchase record, view its price/reference/status and cancel it before fulfillment. An unpaid record lasts 30 minutes. It does not grant a subscription or credits. The record snapshots server prices, royalty percentages and approved direct commission percentages; submitted prices and payment flags are ignored. Request keys protect retries and reject changed payloads. Owner locks limit each account to 20 current unpaid records. Other artists cannot list or cancel these records.

The purchase screen separates the current active package from upcoming paid renewals. Early renewals append a full annual term after existing paid terms, including when a different plan is selected. They do not immediately upgrade or replace the active package. This preserves the repository's renewal convention; immediate upgrades, proration and subscription overlap are not introduced. The subscription API now selects the actual active term, fixing a bug where a later future term could hide current access or show the wrong upload allowance.

Additional studio-generation quotes preserve the current active package price: Starter $3, Growth $2.50 and Professional $2. A verified purchase grants one durable generation credit, with two intended songs subject to provider capability. Included quota is used first. Purchased credits then use the same creator lock, usage reservation, request-key mapping, active-task checks and daily safety limit as included generations. A definitive failed task releases the reservation, making that purchased credit available again. Ambiguous acceptance keeps it reserved. Purchase history and used-credit ownership are protected by unique references and foreign keys. Unused purchased credits are retained and require an active paid package to use; no new expiry or extra-generation affiliate rate is invented.

A product-specific referral can be explicitly attached during subscription review. Its single-use intent is bound to the buyer, plan and order while valid, then consumed in the same transaction as verified subscription fulfillment. Registration creates no commission. Approved direct commissions remain 15% on Starter and 25% on Growth/Professional. Team records represent verified sales volume only. Renewals without a fresh purchase-specific referral create no new commission. Referral intents attached to orders are preserved by cleanup. Historical demo earnings remain intact, but initialization and legacy reporting no longer generate new demo referral earnings merely because an account is active. Legacy activation, demo withdrawal requests and demo administrative reviews are permanently unavailable, including when historical environment switches are set. The historical demo wallet exposes no available cash; retained history stays readable.

## Provider-neutral verification boundary

`lib/commerce.js` accepts an explicitly supplied adapter only in application code. The production application supplies none. The adapter contract is:

- `name`, `merchantId` and reviewed `verified: true` identify the configured provider/merchant. This is a code integration contract, not a runtime activation switch or proof that a real gateway was reviewed.
- `verifyEvent(rawBuffer, headers)` authenticates the original bounded request bytes and returns normalized `{id, paymentId}`. A real adapter must implement the provider's documented signature algorithm, timestamp/replay window, credential selection and bounded parsing. Do not log signing secrets or raw financial payloads.
- `retrievePayment(paymentId)` independently retrieves the authoritative provider payment and returns `{id, orderId, merchantId, status, currency, amountMinor}`. It must enforce timeouts, account scope and verified provider transport. Only final provider status normalized to `paid` can fulfill an order. A browser redirect, client success flag or signed webhook body alone is insufficient.

The service matches payment ID, merchant, immutable order reference, exact integer amount and currency. Unknown, cancelled, expired, mismatched, refunded or otherwise unpaid payments create append-only review events without changing entitlements or balances. Provider lookup failures remain retriable and do not persist successful evidence. An event replay must have the same original-body hash. A second event for an already-fulfilled payment is a duplicate; the payment cannot fulfill another order. Settlement locks the creator, order, event and provider payment, and atomically commits subscription/credit, receipt, balanced journals, approved pending commission, intent consumption and audit. Persistence failure rolls all of these back.

Successful verified receipts create a provider receivable and product-sale record. They do not imply bank settlement. Approved commissions are recorded in a separate pending artist account, never the withdrawable earned account. There is no administrative cash-credit, mark-paid or verification-bypass action. The admin commerce screen shows orders, verification outcomes and accounting discrepancies. Reconciliation compares paid status, receipts, receipt amount/currency, sale journal/transfer and product entitlement. It is an internal consistency check, not reconciliation against an actual bank/provider settlement export. Review events cannot yet be resolved through the UI.

`royaltySplit` calculates approved 50%/60% shares from an explicitly supplied eligible minor-unit amount, conserving the exact total through rounding. It does not determine eligible revenue, fund royalties, derive money from plays or settle paid downloads. Download pricing, eligible-revenue basis, streaming funding/rates, FX, taxes and gateway-fee treatment need approved specifications.

## Safe migration and verification

`003-commerce.sql` adds orders, append-only receipts/event evidence, purchased-generation grants, usage ownership links and immutable purchase snapshots. `004-commerce-integrity.sql` enforces one balanced transfer per journal, subscription ownership, required paid-subscription entitlements and a deferred receipt requirement for purchased credits. Conflicting existing rows stop the migration for reconciliation; no rows are discarded. It does not modify applied migrations 001/002 or import/revalue historical balances. Receipt/event/credit records cannot be edited or deleted. Terminal order records cannot be rewritten. Test this additive migration on a sanitized production clone before an approved release.

Local `npm test` passes 73 behavioral/contract tests plus the existing 25 mocked Sonic cases, 31 source invariants and integrity checks. The new tests cover approved quotes and royalty arithmetic, adapter absence, signature rejection, bounded raw bytes, invalid purchases, retriable provider failures and active/future subscription selection. The integrated PostgreSQL/browser journey passed in [CI run 40193499188](https://github.com/alyeros47-dev/LAE-MUSIC/actions/runs/40193499188), including 27 PostgreSQL checks, real HTTP checks and all three viewports. The final database-constraint and purchase-response checks pass in that run. Desktop/tablet/mobile purchase layouts were visually inspected. Current evidence is recorded in `docs/RELEASE.md` and `artifacts/ci-milestone-3.txt`.

The isolated PostgreSQL journey uses a **test-only** HMAC adapter and independent in-memory authoritative-payment fixture. It exercises the actual service, SQL, ledger and Sonic handler: concurrent fulfillment, wrong amount/currency/merchant/state/ID, signature and replay conflicts, unknown/cancelled/expired/reused payments, contiguous early renewals, fresh-referral commissions, pending-funds withdrawal denial, transactional rollback, purchased-credit consumption/replay/refund, immutable evidence and reconciliation drift. The browser journey reviews/saves/cancels unpaid subscriptions and studio purchases and renders administrative reconciliation at desktop, tablet and phone sizes. None of these tests contacts a real payment provider or receives real money.

## Required before commercial activation

Choose the payment provider/account, merchant country and supported currencies. Complete the actual gateway adapter and hosted checkout/session creation with server-bound order metadata. Implement and review a raw-body webhook route (including a narrowly scoped signature-authenticated exemption from the browser CSRF/origin guard), signature timestamp policy, API timeouts, provider retries/reconciliation jobs, settlement statements, approved commission clearance rules, verified reversals/refunds/disputes and payout/bank verification. Existing public webhook/checkout endpoints must remain unavailable until these are reviewed and verified end to end in the provider sandbox and staging environment. Plan currency is currently USD; there is no implicit NGN conversion.

A paid payment arriving after record expiration/cancellation is deliberately quarantined, not silently accepted or refunded. A second payment for one order and refunds/chargebacks also require reviewed reconciliation/refund workflows. The current candidate cannot safely operate live checkout until these operational paths exist. Commission clearance, funded royalty settlement and bank disbursement remain disabled. Preserved legacy external-upload/lyrics-code and quarterly-team-reward contradictions remain in `docs/AUDIT.md` for explicit business decisions.
