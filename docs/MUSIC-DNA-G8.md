# G8 — African Gospel Choir & Live Worship

Instruction Package 9 adds five individually selectable profiles beneath the
nonselectable **African Gospel Choir & Live Worship** heading. Exact supplied public
names and short descriptions are retained. The library contains 35 styles across
G1–G5, G7 and G8. G6 remains pending its approved specification.

| ID | Name | BPM | Core identity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G8-01 | Heaven's Celebration | 108–132 | Polished African Gospel pop-rock, prominent electric-guitar leads, bright keys, synchronized driving bass and danceable 4/4 |
| G8-02 | Triumphant Praise Assembly | 115–138 | Powerful congregational live praise, punchy bass, dynamic tom rolls, brass, organ stabs and large choir responses |
| G8-03 | Throne Room Harmony | 65–88 | Majestic spacious worship, grand piano, cinematic strings, smooth bass and a controlled full-choir build |
| G8-04 | Sacred Encounter Live | 68–92 | Restrained atmospheric introduction developing into intense live worship, full-band climax and peaceful resolution |
| G8-05 | Joyful Grace Choir | 96–122 | Flowing midtempo African Gospel pop, melodic syncopated bass, electric/acoustic guitars and warm polished choir |

Each profile stores its supplied mood, energy, rhythm, drums, bass, keyboard and guitar
characteristics, additional instruments, melodic direction, arrangement, lyric themes
and production emphasis. Variations change approved hooks, voicings, bass phrases,
choir entrances and instrumental transitions while preserving the core identity.
Voice and supported language combinations remain independent and profiles are gender
neutral. Research references remain private and are excluded from public/provider
projections. No mood selector or new provider parameters are introduced.
