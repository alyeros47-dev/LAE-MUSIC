# S11 — Ghanaian Highlife

Instruction Package 27 adds five selectable profiles beneath the nonselectable
**Secular and Afrobeats / Ghanaian Highlife** heading, separately after S1–S10. Exact
supplied public names/descriptions are retained. The catalogue now contains 125 styles:
70 Gospel and 55 Secular/Afrobeats across S1–S11.

Profiles preserve supplied drum/bass/keyboard/guitar DNA, additional instruments,
rhythm, melody, mood, structure, lyrical themes and bounded variation directions.
Highlife guitar and melodic electric bass remain central rather than generic electronic
Afrobeats. The reggae style retains offbeat guitar and organ bubbles without heavy
dancehall aggression; traditional/romantic arrangements keep natural warmth and dynamics.
S11 remains distinct from S10's Afropop/Hiplife focus while permitting legitimate fusion.

Mood stays internal. No lead gender/register is assigned and supported voices/languages
remain independent. Research references remain private and excluded from public metadata
and provider prompts. Only original melodies, narrative phrases and hooks are requested.

## Language limitations

Current selectable languages remain English, Nigerian Pidgin, Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa,
French and supported combinations. Twi, Ga, Ewe and a distinct Ghanaian Pidgin option
remain unconfigured/unverified. Nigerian Pidgin (`pcm`) is not relabeled as Ghanaian
Pidgin. Ghanaian style never forces Twi or invents unsupported translations. Requested
language additions need subsequent shared-language integration and focused linguistic/
provider checks; this profile-only batch does not advertise those capabilities as active.
