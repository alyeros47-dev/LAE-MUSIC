# R7 — Reggae Gospel

Instruction Package 37 adds five individually selectable profiles under the
nonselectable **Reggae and Caribbean / Reggae Gospel** heading after R1–R6.

| ID | Style | BPM | Distinct musical fingerprint |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R7-01 | Kingdom Roots Worship | 72–96 | Live Jamaican worship band, beat-three one-drop, sustained melodic electric bass, answering organ bubbles and clipped guitar skank |
| R7-02 | Gospel Dancehall Victory | 94–116 | Energetic Caribbean praise riddim, short clean electronic sub-bass, hybrid kick/snare/claps, synth plucks and rhythmic organ stabs |
| R7-03 | Street Testimony Reggae | 88–108 | Firm hybrid groove with occasional one-drop contrast, organic roots bass motifs, organ/piano, blues guitar and narrative testimony space |
| R7-04 | Heavenly Reggae Soul | 68–92 | Comforting gospel lovers rock, gentle one-drop, flowing warm bass, acoustic piano/Rhodes, soft acoustic guitar and gospel chord extensions |
| R7-05 | African Gospel Reggae Fire | 76–102 | Nigerian gospel roots/steppers, rolling electric bass, organ bubbles, Highlife-influenced guitar and talking-drum/shekere accents |

Profiles preserve supplied public descriptions, tempos, embedded moods, drum/bass/
keyboard/guitar DNA, additional instruments, melody, arrangement, lyrical guidance
and production safeguards. Christian gospel character remains explicit while the
customer's topic determines the lyrical direction. Meaningful biblical expression,
clear testimony structure when appropriate, original melody and vocal-friendly
instrumental space remain priorities; no guaranteed individual outcomes are promised.

Voice and supported languages remain independent. Gospel Dancehall never requires
a rapid deejay persona; rhythmic singing or spoken-style passages must fit the
requested performance. Heavenly Reggae Soul supports any selected voice, and optional
backing harmonies respect customer settings. Neither Patois, Nigerian Pidgin nor a
regional accent is forced. Research references remain private and excluded from
public metadata/provider prompts; identifiable imitation is prohibited.

Existing English, Nigerian Pidgin, Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, French and supported
combinations remain selectable. Jamaican Patois and arbitrary custom language codes
remain unsupported by the shared registry; no new provider capability is claimed.
Additional languages require separate integration and linguistic/provider review.
