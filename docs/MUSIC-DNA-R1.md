# R1 — Classic Roots Reggae

Instruction Package 31 adds five individually selectable profiles under the
nonselectable **Reggae and Caribbean / Classic Roots Reggae** heading, beginning
this department without changing any earlier category or profile.

| ID | Style | BPM | Distinctive musical foundation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1-01 | Island Freedom Roots | 72–94 | Relaxed one-drop with beat-three kick/rimshot, sustained syncopated electric bass, organ bubbles and uplifting communal melody |
| R1-02 | Rebel Roots Declaration | 70–92 | Heavy one-drop or restrained steppers, deliberate low-register bass motifs, sharp skank, blues guitar bends and brass stabs |
| R1-03 | Ancient Drum Roots | 66–86 | Spacious slow one-drop, hypnotic sustained bass, Nyabinghi-influenced hand drums, dark organ and developing chant-like refrains |
| R1-04 | Harmony of the People | 72–96 | Flowing melodic bass, natural one-drop, bubbling organ, rhythmic piano and communal melodic arrangements compatible with the selected voice |
| R1-05 | Kingston Mystic Groove | 70–94 | Relaxed one-drop, soulful melodic bass runs/slides, expressive organ, gentle guitar replies and restrained dub effects |

All profiles preserve supplied descriptions, drum/bass/keyboard/guitar details,
additional instruments, melody, arrangements, suggested lyrical themes, built-in
moods and avoidance guidance. Deep electric bass stays audible without overpowering
other instruments. Bounded variation changes permitted introductions, chord voicings,
arrangement details, melodic directions and original lyrical expression. A new
request can vary within the family; retries retain their existing variation identity.
No provider seed parameter or guaranteed audio uniqueness is claimed.

Voice and language stay independent. Harmony of the People permits backing layers
only when the customer's vocal settings permit them; a single-voice request never
requires harmonies. Ancient Drum Roots' contemplative atmosphere does not impose
beliefs. No profile forces Rastafarian, religious or political content. Research names
remain internal and absent from public options/provider instructions; identifiable
imitation is prohibited.

Jamaican Patois is not available in the existing shared registry. No unverified
language option is added in this catalogue-only batch. Existing English, Nigerian
Pidgin, Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, French and supported combinations remain available.
Patois needs a separate language integration and linguistic/provider verification.
