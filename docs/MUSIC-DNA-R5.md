Profiles preserve supplied public labels/descriptions, tempos, moods, drums, bass,
keyboards, guitars, additional instruments, melodies, arrangements and production
safeguards. Heavy clear bass, punchy kicks, sharp snares and Jamaican riddims remain
central rather than generic Afrobeats, trap or EDM. Club/explosive profiles do not
inherit unrelated mandatory roots one-drop drums or organ. Optional double-time
subdivisions for Kingston Club Pressure do not double its approved BPM range or
invent provider parameters.

Mood remains internal and voice/language settings independent. Ragga Lovers Party
Groove never automatically adds a duet or multiple performers. Classic call-and-
response opportunities respect selected vocal settings. Topic-appropriate writing
does not automatically introduce explicit sexual content, violence or profanity;
playful storytelling never forces comedy for a serious topic. Research references
remain private and absent from public metadata/provider prompts; identifiable
imitation is prohibited.

Jamaican Patois remains unavailable in the shared language registry. Existing
English, French, Nigerian Pidgin, Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa and supported combinations
remain selectable without forcing Patois. A separate language integration and
linguistic/provider verification are required before advertising Patois support.
