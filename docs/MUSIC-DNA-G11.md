# G11 — South African Gospel & Choir

Instruction Package 12 adds five individually selectable profiles beneath the
nonselectable **South African Gospel & Choir** heading. Exact supplied public names and
descriptions are retained. The library now contains 50 styles across G1–G5 and G7–G11;
G6 remains pending its specification.

| ID | Name | BPM | Defining character |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G11-01 | Celebration Choir Explosion | 112–138 | Full multi-part ensemble, SATB-style harmony layers, bouncy synchronized bass, powerful drums and communal dance responses |
| G11-02 | Royal Worship Gathering | 72–98 | Spacious majestic live worship, expressive grand piano, rich organ, warm bass and natural choir-supported dynamics |
| G11-03 | African Heritage Praise | 104–130 | Traditional South African praise, communal clapping, rolling bass, live percussion and melodic group responses |
| G11-04 | Healing Presence Worship | 66–90 | Gentle soulful piano worship, sustained warm bass, ambient electric guitar, layered choir and controlled emotional development |
| G11-05 | Spirit of Harmony | 74–102 | Expressive Gospel soul, sophisticated choir harmony, rich keyboards, flowing syncopated bass and natural live-band movement |

Each profile preserves supplied drum/bass/keyboard DNA, instruments, choir arrangement,
mood, rhythm, melody, song structure, lyrics and bounded creative-variation directions.
Production guidance retains South African musical character rather than generic
Afro-Gospel, Nigerian praise or Western worship. Traditional praise explicitly avoids
conversion into generic electronic dance music. Rich ensemble harmonies remain separate
from the customer's independent lead voice: SATB-style describes multi-part choir
arrangement and does not assign the lead's gender. No mood selector is introduced.
Research references remain private and are excluded from public metadata and prompts.

Existing English, Pidgin, Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, French and supported combinations remain
available independently. isiZulu, isiXhosa and Sesotho are not currently selectable or
verified in the shared registry. The conditional “where available” specification does
not activate new languages or provider capabilities. These need future shared language
integration and linguistic verification; no South African language is forced here.

`npm run test:dna -- --groups G11` passes 14 focused G11 tests and 7 CI-routing checks.
Tests cover exact metadata and tempos, individual selection and nonselectable heading,
private references, distinct instrument/arrangement cores, independent lead voices and
languages, fixed mood, bounded variation, stable retries and actual lyrics-handler
prompt construction with synthetic provider replies. Successful requests reserve and
complete one credit. All 45 earlier profiles remain unchanged; no shared engine, voice,
provider, billing or financial code changed. Broad earlier-group, unrelated platform,
database and browser suites are not repeated.

No paid calls, real audio verification, merge or deployment occurred. Text contracts do
not certify audio originality, ensemble quality, cultural fidelity or language accuracy.
