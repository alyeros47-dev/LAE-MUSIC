# LAE-MUSIC Music Creator Agreement

Version: 2026-11-05-v1

## Your membership

LAE-MUSIC provides AI-powered music creation, streaming and creator monetization services. Annual artist packages are Starter ($6: 3 generations, up to 6 tracks), Growth ($10: 6 generations, up to 12 tracks) and Premium ($20: 15 generations, up to 30 tracks). Each generation is intended to produce two separate tracks, each with its own account-linked, one-time publishing code. Provider failures remain subject to the existing credit-restoration and review procedures. Additional generation prices must be approved and shown before purchase.

## Your creative contributions

You may supply original lyrics and production instructions. You must own or have permission to use your lyrics, recordings, voice samples, artwork and other contributions. Use another person's voice only with their permission. Do not submit infringing, unlawful or prohibited content. AI-generated material is not automatically copyright-free; applicable copyright and licensing requirements still apply.

## Publishing and platform permission

Only recordings generated through LAE-MUSIC Studio may be newly published during this launch. Each publication must be linked to your completed generation record. When you publish, you authorize LAE-MUSIC to store, display, stream and distribute that recording and its associated artwork and metadata through the platform to provide its services. This permission does not transfer ownership of your contributions. Releases remain subject to existing moderation, copyright complaints and takedown procedures.

## Renewal and expiry

Membership lasts one year. An early renewal begins after the current paid term; a renewal after expiry begins when fulfilled. New generation and publishing require an active membership and available allowance. If membership expires, existing published songs remain available and eligible streaming activity may continue earning royalties. Dashboard access, account records and existing earnings remain accessible. Expiry does not delete songs or waive content rules. Included allowances belong to the relevant membership year. Unused purchased additional generations remain recorded and can be used after renewal; an active membership is required. Previously recorded benefits are preserved.

## Royalties and renewal deductions

Revenue is earned only from eligible activity under the applicable financial terms and reconciliation procedures; income is not guaranteed. This agreement does not set or change royalty shares, affiliate rates or payouts. Accepting it does not authorize a charge. Paying a renewal from available royalties requires a separate explicit confirmation of the package, exact USD amount and deduction for that renewal only. There is no recurring royalty deduction or automatic renewal authorization. Pending, held, unreconciled or already reserved funds cannot be spent. If sufficient eligible funds are unavailable, no deduction or renewal occurs.

## Records, changes and refunds

LAE-MUSIC records the agreement version, its content hash and your acceptance time against your account. You can review these records in your membership dashboard. A changed agreement requires fresh acceptance before new creation or publishing. Existing refund, complaint, privacy and account-security procedures continue to apply. No provision here guarantees income, waives applicable consumer rights or grants permission to use third-party intellectual property without authorization.
