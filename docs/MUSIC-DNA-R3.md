nonselectable **Reggae and Caribbean / Romantic Reggae & Lovers Rock** heading.

Each profile retains supplied labels/descriptions, tempos, mood, drums, bass,
keyboards, guitar, other instruments, melody, arrangement, lyrical themes and
production safeguards. Clear deep bass and reggae rhythm survive soul, R&B and
pop influences. The fifth profile follows its supplied polished reggae-pop drums;
it is not incorrectly restricted to the first four profiles' one-drop kit or
exclusively electric bass. Both approved bass alternatives are validated.

Mood stays internal; voice and supported languages remain independent. Optional
backing layers respect single-voice, duet and supported harmony settings. Romantic
musical atmosphere does not force a fixed love-story subject. Gospel-influenced
harmony in Golden Heart describes musical chords and arrangement, not automatic
religious lyrics. Artist research references remain private, excluded from public
metadata and provider instructions; identifiable imitation is prohibited.

Jamaican Patois remains unavailable in the shared language registry. This data-only
package does not add unverified provider support. Existing English, French, Nigerian
Pidgin, Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa and supported combinations remain available without
forcing Patois. Additional language support requires a separate integration and
linguistic/provider verification.
