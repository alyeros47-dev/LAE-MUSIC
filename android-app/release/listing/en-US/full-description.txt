Find your sound on LAE-MUSIC.

Swipe through full-screen music artwork in Discover. Choose Gospel, secular music or both, and shape your recommendations as you listen.

For Gospel, choose Praise, Worship, Gospel Reggae, Choir, Revival, African Gospel or All Gospel. Browse Home and Explore for featured artists, trending songs and recent releases.

Listen with your screen locked. Use your phone's media controls to play, pause and change tracks. Like songs, follow artists, share music and join the conversation.

Keep your music together with liked songs, playlists and listening history. Premium listeners can save eligible songs for protected playback inside the app. Saved songs stay on your device until you remove them or uninstall. A daily internet connection is currently needed to refresh listening access; an expired licence temporarily locks playback without deleting the saved file.

Use your existing LAE-MUSIC listening account. Music creation, publishing and artist business tools remain on the LAE-MUSIC website.
