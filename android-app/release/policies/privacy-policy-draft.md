# LAE-MUSIC Android privacy policy — DRAFT, NOT PUBLISHED

Operator: LAE MUSIC VENTURE. Contact: laemusic067@gmail.com.

This draft covers the listening app. The owner must approve it, confirm the business address, retention/deletion rules and website processors, and publish a stable public HTTPS page before Google Play submission. It must also be accessible inside the app. This file is not a live policy URL.

## Information used

We use your LAE-MUSIC account details to sign you in and link listening access to your existing account. Passwords travel over HTTPS to the existing sign-in service; the app stores protected session credentials, not a copy of your password.

Our services receive requests for songs and artwork, likes, follows, comments, playlists, reports, subscription checks and listening history. Valid listening activity is recorded under the platform's existing royalty rules. Network requests also expose connection information such as IP address to the hosting services.

Music preferences and recommendation summaries are stored on this device, separately for each account and guest listening. They include selected music categories, actual listening duration/completion, early skips, repeat listening and confirmed likes, comments and saves. This app does not send these new preference/ranking summaries to an analytics service. Existing online social actions and valid-play requests still reach LAE-MUSIC. Music choices are used for music recommendations, not to infer personal religious identity or target advertisements. Reset recommendations in Profile to clear local recommendation summaries; change Music preferences to revise choices.

Premium downloads use an account/device-bound listening key, signed access grants and encrypted app-private files. Files survive normal restarts and reboots. Access currently needs daily online renewal and remains subject to subscription expiry. Removing a download deletes its local media. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes local data, including saved music.

## Sharing and service providers

Public comments and artist-facing interactions may be visible to other listeners. A shared song link is sent through the app you choose. LAE-MUSIC's existing hosting and service providers process requests to provide the service. The owner must confirm the complete processor list and any other permitted sharing in the final policy. There is no Firebase, advertising ID, advertising SDK, location collection, contacts permission, microphone recorder, or voice training in this listening app.

Google Play Billing is disabled in the current preview. Before enabling purchases, update this policy and Data Safety declarations to describe Google's purchase processing, server-side receipt verification, transaction records and subscription synchronization. Card or bank credentials must not be collected by this app.

## Choices and deletion

You can sign out, remove saved music, reset local recommendations, change music choices and contact support. Signing out does not erase server accounts or financial records. A working in-app and public account-deletion request process is still required before publication. The final policy must describe what is erased, what legally required financial/fraud records are retained, why, and for how long. No retention periods have been invented here.

## Protection and age suitability

The app uses HTTPS, Android Keystore protection for local session secrets and encrypted offline audio. No service can promise absolute protection. The owner must finalize target age groups, child-access rules and moderation policies before publication; this draft makes no unsupported age or legal compliance claim.
